AiRA Teams Win Awards at HUST Student Research Conference 2026

aira-lab May 16, 2026
award conference student-research robotics origami svnckh

We are thrilled to announce that two AiRA Lab student teams have been recognized at the 43rd HUST Annual Student Research Conference (SVNCKH), held at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST) on May 15–16, 2026.

This year’s conference — themed “Student Month of Science, Technology & Innovation” — brought together outstanding undergraduate research projects across all departments of HUST, with our teams competing in the Electronics and Mechatronics Engineering category.

Our Achievements

Robotics Team — 3rd Prize

Our Robotics team earned the prestigious Third Prize, recognizing their excellent research contributions and technical innovation in robotic systems.

Origami-Inspired Technology Team — Outstanding Creative Idea Award

Our Origami-Inspired Technology team was honored with the Outstanding Creative Idea Award, celebrating their novel and distinctive approach to applying origami-inspired mechanisms in engineering design.

Looking Ahead

These achievements reflect the dedication and creativity of our students, and reinforce AiRA Lab’s commitment to nurturing the next generation of researchers and engineers. We are proud of every team member who contributed to this success.

Congratulations to all award recipients — we look forward to seeing your work grow into future publications and innovations!
