Nguyen Dang Doanh is a third-year undergraduate student in the Department of Mechatronics at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and a member of the Dynamics & Control group at the AiRA Laboratory.

Doanh's research focuses on soft robotics, including the modeling and control of soft and continuum robotic systems.
