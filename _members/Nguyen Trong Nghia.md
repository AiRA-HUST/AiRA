Nguyen Trong Nghia is a third-year undergraduate student in the Department of Mechatronics at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and a member of the Embodied AI group at the AiRA Laboratory.

Nghia's research focuses on aerial robotics, specifically the simulation of an octocopter (octodrone). Nghia builds and validates drone models across MuJoCo, Gazebo (ROS 2 / PX4 SITL), and Isaac Sim, comparing simulation fidelity and speed as a foundation for sim-to-real transfer.
