Pham Minh Duy is a third-year undergraduate student at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and a member of the AiRA Laboratory.

Duy's research focuses on structure design for Kresling origami and DNA origami. Duy is particularly interested in origami-inspired mechanical intelligence, where stable states, snap-through transitions, and multistability of origami cells are used to store and process information through mechanical deformation, and in AI-driven methods that synthesize origami geometry from a target behavior.
