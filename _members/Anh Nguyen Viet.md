Anh Viet Nguyen is a fifth-year student in the Department of Mechatronics at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology. He is an active member of the AiRA Laboratory, where he focuses on the intersection of artificial intelligence and robotics learning systems.

His research interests center on AI-driven robotics, machine learning for autonomous systems, and intelligent robot learning algorithms. As part of the AiRA team, Anh is involved in developing learning-based approaches for robotic systems, including reinforcement learning for robot control, neural network architectures for perception and decision-making, and adaptive algorithms that enable robots to learn from experience and improve their performance over time.

His current research addresses efficient and reliable diffusion policies for real-time robot grasping on the SO101-ARM platform using LeRobot. He also mentors undergraduate members of the Embodied AI group and collaborates on human-in-the-loop robotics, where EEG error-related potentials (ErrP) from human observers are used to help ACT policies detect and correct mistakes early.
