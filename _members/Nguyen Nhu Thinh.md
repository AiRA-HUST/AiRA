Nguyen Nhu Thinh is an undergraduate student in the Department of Mechatronics at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and a member of the AiRA Laboratory.

His research focuses on physics-informed machine learning for mechanical and origami-inspired systems. He studies nonlinear vibration analysis of Kresling origami structures and contributes to inverse design methods using physics-informed neural networks.

Thinh is also involved in research on composite laminates, where he explores the use of PINN-based approaches for topology optimization in composite design problems.
