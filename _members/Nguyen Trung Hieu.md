Nguyen Trung Hieu is a third-year undergraduate student in the Department of Mechatronics at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and a member of the Dynamics & Control group at the AiRA Laboratory.

Hieu's research focuses on reinforcement learning for robotics. As part of the MicroDuck project, Hieu implements reinforcement learning algorithms on real robot hardware, covering hardware integration, real-time control, and deployment of learned policies beyond simulation.
