Duc Vu Minh is an alumnus of the AiRA Laboratory and a graduate of the Department of Mechatronics at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology. After graduating, he joined Viettel High Tech as an intern.

During his time at AiRA, Duc specialized in robotic arm development and autonomous action learning. His graduation project built a LeRobot SO-101 ARM system for object grasping, covering dynamics modeling and mechanical analysis of the arm, controller design (PID, model predictive control, and robust control), operational data collection, and training of ACT (Action Chunking with Transformers) policies for grasping tasks. He also worked with Anh Nguyen Viet on diffusion policy optimization for real-time robot grasping.
