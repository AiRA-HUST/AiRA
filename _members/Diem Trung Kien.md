Diem Trung Kien is a second-year undergraduate student in the Mechatronics program at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and a member of the AiRA Laboratory.

Kien works on the lab's NanoRobot project, which designs nanoscale robotic structures based on DNA origami. Kien is currently learning coarse-grained molecular simulation with oxDNA to model the structure and mechanical behavior of DNA origami nanostructures.
