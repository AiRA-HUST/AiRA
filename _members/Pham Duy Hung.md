Pham Duy Hung is a third-year undergraduate student in the Department of Mechatronics at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and a member of the Embodied AI group at the AiRA Laboratory.

Hung works with the LeRobot framework on mobile manipulation, combining the LeKiwi mobile base with the SO101-ARM, with interests in vision-based robot manipulation.
