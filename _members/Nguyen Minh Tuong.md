Nguyen Minh Tuong is a third-year undergraduate student in the Department of Mechatronics at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and a member of the AiRA Laboratory.

His research focuses on intelligent predictive control for dynamic robotic systems: how robots can exploit physics, data, and online feedback to act reliably when their models and environments are imperfect. He develops and evaluates model predictive control (MPC) for cart-pole and inverted pendulum systems in MuJoCo, and is interested in verified neural network control and learning-based models for imperfect dynamics.
