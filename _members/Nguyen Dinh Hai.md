Nguyen Dinh Hai is a third-year undergraduate student in the Department of Mechatronics at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and a member of the AiRA Laboratory.

His topics include computer vision, robotics, and world models. He currently works with the LeRobot SO101-ARM platform and is moving toward deploying vision-language-action (VLA) and video-action world models on edge devices and mobile manipulators.
