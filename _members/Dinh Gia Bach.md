Dinh Gia Bach is a third-year undergraduate student in the Department of Mechatronics at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and a member of the Embodied AI group at the AiRA Laboratory.

Bach is building a two-arm teleoperation rig for collecting demonstration data used to train bimanual manipulation policies in simulation. Bach's work covers hardware fabrication, teleoperation and data-collection pipelines, and MuJoCo scene setup, with imitation learning as the next step.
