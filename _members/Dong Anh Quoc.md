Dong Anh Quoc is a fourth-year undergraduate student in the Department of Mechatronics at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and a member of the AiRA Laboratory.

His research topics include brain–computer interfaces (BCI), EEG signal processing, and robotics applications that leverage neural decoding for intelligent control. He is developing an EEG setup to measure error-related potentials (ErrP), the brain signals that appear when a person observes a mistake, and uses them in a human-in-the-loop framework that helps robot policies such as ACT detect erroneous behavior early and correct it. This work is carried out in collaboration with VNU Vietnam Japan University (VJU).
