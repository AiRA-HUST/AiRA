Khuat Hung Anh is a third-year undergraduate student in the Department of Mechatronics at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and a member of the Dynamics & Control group at the AiRA Laboratory.

Hung Anh's research integrates soft, compliant tools and grippers with rigid robot platforms such as humanoids and manipulators, enabling safe handling of fragile and deformable objects in industrial and everyday settings. Hung Anh uses the SOFA framework for finite-element modeling and real-time simulation of soft robots, and is part of the Computational Soft Robotics (CSR) team collaborating with RMIT University.
